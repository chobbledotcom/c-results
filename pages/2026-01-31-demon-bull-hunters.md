🐂 Demon Bull Hunters – Battle Cards

Train. Battle. Level Up.

Demon Bull Hunters is a real-world martial arts battle card system where fitness, skill and strategy collide.

This isn’t just a card game.
And it isn’t just training.

It’s a physical, screen-free battle experience where players become fighters, complete challenges, and use their training to power up their deck.

🥊 HOW IT WORKS

Players collect character cards, technique cards, challenge cards and fortune cards to build their battle deck.

But here’s the twist…

⚡ You don’t just play the moves — you perform them.

Every battle involves:

Fitness challenges

Martial arts techniques

Dice rolls

Strategy

Real movement

If a card says 20 jabs… you throw them.
If you lose a roll… you owe reps.
If a raid happens… everyone defends.

Your body becomes part of the game.

🎮 WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

Most games level up characters.

Demon Bull Hunters levels up the player.

✔ Builds fitness
✔ Improves coordination
✔ Develops discipline
✔ Encourages effort
✔ Boosts confidence
✔ Teaches martial arts skills

Kids don’t even realise how hard they’re working — they just want to win battles and unlock new cards.

🎖 PROGRESSION SYSTEM

The card system follows martial arts belt progression.

As players train and pass gradings, they unlock:

Higher belt character cards

Advanced techniques

Special challenges

Boss battles

Limited raid cards

Training = Power.
Effort = Better cards.

🐉 RAID BOSSES & EVENTS

Special sessions include raid bosses, chaos cards, and event battles where players team up to defeat powerful enemies.

These days are loud, energetic and unforgettable — the gym turns into a live action game arena.

🌍 SCREEN-FREE. ACTIVE. SOCIAL.

In a world of tablets and consoles, Demon Bull Hunters gets players:

Off screens

On their feet

Working together

Competing in a healthy way

Laughing while exercising

It’s part game.
Part workout.
Part martial arts journey.

💥 WHO IT’S FOR

Perfect for:

Kids who love games

Kids who need motivation to exercise

Martial arts students

Schools & youth groups

Competitive personalities

Kids who struggle with focus in normal sports

Demon Bull Hunters isn’t about sitting and playing.
It’s about moving, battling, improving and becoming stronger — in the game and in real life.

🐂 Enter the arena. Earn your power.
