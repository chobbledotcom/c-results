## Train. Battle. Level Up.

Demon Bull Hunters is a real-world martial arts battle card system where fitness, skill and strategy collide.

This isn't just a card game. And it isn't just training.

It's a physical, screen-free battle experience where players become fighters, complete challenges, and use their training to power up their deck.

### How it works

Players collect character cards, technique cards, challenge cards and fortune cards to build their battle deck.

But here's the twist — you don't just play the moves, you perform them.

Every battle involves:

- Fitness challenges
- Martial arts techniques
- Dice rolls
- Strategy
- Real movement

If a card says 20 jabs… you throw them. If you lose a roll… you owe reps. If a raid happens… everyone defends.

Your body becomes part of the game.

### What makes it different

Most games level up characters. Demon Bull Hunters levels up the player.

Kids don't even realise how hard they're working — they just want to win battles and unlock new cards.

### Progression system

The card system follows martial arts belt progression. As players train and pass gradings, they unlock:

- Higher belt character cards
- Advanced techniques
- Special challenges
- Boss battles
- Limited raid cards

Training = Power. Effort = Better cards.

### Raid bosses and events

Special sessions include raid bosses, chaos cards, and event battles where players team up to defeat powerful enemies. These days are loud, energetic and unforgettable — the gym turns into a live action game arena.

### Screen-free, active, social

In a world of tablets and consoles, Demon Bull Hunters gets players off screens, on their feet, working together, competing in a healthy way, and laughing while exercising.

It's part game. Part workout. Part martial arts journey.

### Who it's for

- Kids who love games
- Kids who need motivation to exercise
- Martial arts students
- Schools and youth groups
- Competitive personalities
- Kids who struggle with focus in normal sports

Demon Bull Hunters isn't about sitting and playing. It's about moving, battling, improving and becoming stronger — in the game and in real life.
