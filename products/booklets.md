## Booklets - When You Need More Than a Flyer

Carl here. Need to get detailed information across? That's where booklets come in handy. I print everything from 8-page service guides to 100-page product catalogues, all bound properly so they don't fall apart.

### How I Bind Them

Saddle stitching is your standard booklet binding - two staples down the middle, perfect for most booklets up to 40 pages. Want something that looks more like a real book? Perfect binding gives you that square spine, looks the business for thick catalogues. Wire binding with spiral coils means they lay flat - brilliant for training manuals people actually use while working. And if you need something extra durable that'll get hammered daily, loop stitching keeps everything secure.

### What I've Done Recently

Just finished a 48-page course booklet for a dog training school - wire bound so owners can have it open while training their dogs. Did 500 product catalogues for a bathroom supplier, perfect bound, looked really professional. Also sorted event programs for Thornbury carnival - 16 pages, saddle stitched, delivered two days before they needed them.

### Popular Uses

Product catalogues let you show everything you sell in one professional booklet. Training manuals work brilliantly for staff induction, customer guides, or course participants. Event programs for weddings, shows, and conferences always go down well. Service guides that properly explain what you do help close more sales. Company handbooks get new staff up to speed quickly and consistently.

### Why Booklets Work Better

More professional than stapling [flyers](/products/flyers-leaflets) together. More detailed than [brochures](/products/brochures). And unlike sending people to your [website](/products/website-build-hosting), they've got something physical to keep and refer back to.
